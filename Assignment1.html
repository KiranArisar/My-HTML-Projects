My Favourite Hobby: Reading Books

Reading opens the door to knowledge and imagination.

About Reading

My favourite hobby is reading books. I enjoy reading novels, biographies, and educational books. Reading improves my vocabulary, knowledge, and creativity.

Benefits of Reading

  • Improves vocabulary
  • Increases knowledge
  • Reduces stress
  • Improves concentration

My Reading Routine

  1. Choose an interesting book.
  2. Find a quiet place to read.
  3. Read for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Write down important ideas.

Types of Books

Novel
A long fictional story with characters and events.
Biography
A book about the life of a real person.
Self-help Book
A book that provides advice for personal growth.
